# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Shuddhi Website

## 1. Business Summary
Shuddhi is a premium D2C lifestyle brand based at 6/316 Jankipuram Extension, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, 226021, India (Mobile: +91 73553 21473), crafting elegant, reusable copper drinkware inspired by traditional Indian wisdom and modern design. Operating across Central Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Sitapur, Hardoi, Raebareli, Barabanki, Unnao) and Pan-India, Shuddhi empowers health-conscious consumers to replace single-use plastic bottles with timeless copper vessels. Anchored by its flagship 1 Litre Classic Copper Bottle at ₹799, the brand combines wellness education, authentic craftsmanship, and conscious consumption to drive direct-to-consumer online sales.

## 2. Target Visitor & Primary Concerns
- **Visitor Profile**: Health-conscious men, women, and parents (aged 22–45) in urban and semi-urban India, Ayurveda/wellness enthusiasts, and environmentally conscious shoppers looking for safe family hydration and meaningful gifts.
- **Key Visitor Concerns**: 
  - Harmful microplastics and chemical leaching from single-use plastic bottles.
  - Verification of 100% pure copper metal quality and safety.
  - Difficulty in daily cleaning, maintenance, and natural copper tarnish.
  - Product durability, fair pricing, and fast local delivery from Lucknow across UP and India.
  - Fear of exaggerated, pseudo-scientific health gimmicks.

## 3. The Six Pages & Their Single Jobs
1. **Home (`index.html`)**: Instantly communicate brand positioning (*"Purity in Every Sip"*), showcase flagship bottles, build immediate trust, and guide visitors toward shopping.
2. **Shop (`shop.html`)**: Provide an intuitive product grid with filtering and sorting options to help visitors compare finishes (Classic, Hammered, Matte, Premium) and capacities (500ml, 750ml, 1L, Gift Sets).
3. **Product Detail (`product.html`)**: Deliver complete SKU details, multi-image galleries, transparent pricing, SKU care instructions, and drive immediate cart addition and checkout conversion.
4. **Why Copper (`why-copper.html`)**: Educate visitors on traditional Indian copper vessel heritage, everyday wellness context, and responsible cleaning routines without medical hyperbole.
5. **Sustainability (`sustainability.html`)**: Highlight Shuddhi's plastic-free mission, contrast single-use plastic waste against reusable copper durability, and explain conscious packaging.
6. **About Us (`about.html`)**: Share the founder's story, Lucknow craft origin, brand core values, and foster deep emotional credibility and trust.

## 4. The Single Primary Action
- **Primary Conversion**: Visitor clicks **"Shop Now" / "Buy Now"** and completes a purchase of a Shuddhi copper bottle.

## 5. Information Required to Earn a Parent's Trust
- Assurance of 100% pure copper craftsmanship with zero plastic contact in the body.
- Clear, practical care instructions (gentle cleaning with lemon/salt, dry thoroughly, avoid dishwasher).
- Responsible wellness language accompanied by an explicit disclaimer: *"Shuddhi products are lifestyle products and are not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any medical condition."*
- Authentic brand headquarters in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, transparent shipping/return policies, and accessible customer support.
- Real, verified purchaser feedback and clear pricing transparency.

## 6. What We Must NOT Include
- Exaggerated or illegal medical claims (*"cures diseases", "prevents cancer", "treats diabetes", "guaranteed immunity boost"*).
- Fabricated certifications or seals (*fake ISO, BIS, FDA, or "100% medical grade"*).
- Invented customer reviews or unconfirmed SKU prices.
- Heavy JavaScript frameworks, external UI libraries, complex build steps, or cluttered generic templates.
- Exposed payment secrets or hidden fees.
