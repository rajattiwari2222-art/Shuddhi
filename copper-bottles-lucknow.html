Why Choose Shuddhi in Lucknow?

In the fast-paced life of Lucknow, staying hydrated is part of a mindful daily routine. Our 1-litre pure copper bottles are reusable drinkware inspired by Ayurveda’s traditional use of copper vessels.

Unlike disposable plastic, copper vessels have long been associated with storing drinking water and are traditionally valued for their antimicrobial properties. Shuddhi brings that household tradition into a modern bottle designed for everyday use.
