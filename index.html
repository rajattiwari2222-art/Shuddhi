TRADITION & DAILY USE

Why Copper?

Copper vessels have been used in Indian households for generations. Shuddhi takes inspiration from this traditional practice and brings it into a modern, reusable bottle designed for everyday use.

Learn More About Copper →

Note: Shuddhi copper bottles are lifestyle products designed for general everyday reusable hydration. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
RESPONSIBLE LIVING

Choose Reusable.

A reusable bottle is a simple way to reduce dependence on disposable plastic in everyday life.

By choosing a durable copper vessel built for years of daily use, you take a conscious step toward a less disposable lifestyle.

Read Our Philosophy
OUR STORY

Shuddhi started with a simple idea: make everyday hydration more conscious.

We wanted to bring the traditional use of copper vessels into a modern lifestyle while encouraging people to move away from disposable plastic bottles.

That idea became Shuddhi. Today, we focus on creating copper bottles that are practical, reusable and thoughtfully designed for everyday use.
