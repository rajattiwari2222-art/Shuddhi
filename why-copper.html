CARE GUIDE

Cleaning & Maintaining Your Bottle

Pure copper naturally oxidizes over time when exposed to air and moisture, forming a dark patina. This is a natural characteristic of pure copper.

Simple Lemon & Salt Method

To clean natural tarnish:

  1. 1. Mix equal parts fresh lemon juice (or vinegar) and table salt.
  2. 2. Rub gently over the bottle using a soft cloth.
  3. 3. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
  4. 4. Wipe completely dry with a soft towel.
