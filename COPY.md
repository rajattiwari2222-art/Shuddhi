### Configurable Care Instructions
1. **First Use**: Clean thoroughly with warm water and mild liquid soap before filling with drinking water.
2. **Daily Routine**: Rinse daily. Fill with plain water for 6–8 hours for traditional overnight storage.
3. **Natural Tarnishing**: Pure copper naturally oxidizes over time when exposed to air and moisture, forming a dark patina. This is a hallmark of genuine copper.
4. **Natural Cleaning Method**: To restore shine, mix equal parts lemon juice (or vinegar) and table salt. Rub gently over the bottle surface using a soft cloth, rinse thoroughly with clean water, and wipe completely dry.
5. **Precautions**: Do not use abrasive scrubbers. Do not put in a dishwasher. Use primarily for plain drinking water (avoid acidic beverages like citrus juices, milk, or carbonated drinks).

### The Legacy of Copper Vessels in India
For centuries, households across India have relied on copper vessels to store drinking water. Rooted in traditional Ayurvedic practices, water stored in copper vessels overnight was prized as part of a balanced daily morning routine.

### Recognized Antimicrobial Properties
Modern research acknowledges that copper possesses inherent antimicrobial properties. Storing clean water in copper vessels helps maintain freshness naturally over time.

### How to Use Your Copper Bottle Responsibly
- Fill your Shuddhi bottle with fresh drinking water at night.
- Allow the water to rest in the bottle overnight at room temperature.
- Drink a glass of fresh copper-stored water in the morning.
