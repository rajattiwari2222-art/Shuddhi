About Shuddhi

Shuddhi started with a simple idea: make everyday hydration more conscious.

BACKGROUND

Thoughtfully Designed for Everyday Use

We wanted to bring the traditional use of copper vessels into a modern lifestyle while encouraging people to move away from disposable plastic bottles.

That idea became Shuddhi. Today, we focus on creating copper bottles that are practical, reusable and thoughtfully designed for everyday use.
